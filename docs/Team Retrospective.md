# Team Retrospective – Data Diet Project

## Overview

As part of the project closure phase, the team conducted a retrospective meeting to reflect on the overall project experience, evaluate team performance, identify challenges encountered during development, and discuss opportunities for improvement in future projects.

---

## What Worked Well as a Team?

Several factors contributed to the success of the Data Diet project:

* Effective communication and collaboration among team members throughout the project lifecycle.
* Clear distribution of roles and responsibilities, which improved accountability and productivity.
* Rapid problem-solving and issue resolution whenever challenges arose.
* Continuous follow-up and coordination by the Project Manager, helping the team remain aligned with project goals and deadlines.
* Strong collaboration when addressing technical challenges and integrating project components.
* Team members actively supported one another during periods of increased workload and project pressure.

These practices created a productive working environment and enabled the team to successfully deliver all planned MVP features.

---

## What Challenges Did We Face and How Were They Resolved?

### Challenges Faced

* Managing source code integration and version control throughout development.
* Resolving merge conflicts when combining work from multiple branches.
* Maintaining clear communication while multiple features were being developed simultaneously.
* Ensuring consistency in design and user experience across different pages and modules.
* Coordinating dependencies between frontend and backend development tasks.
* Ensuring smooth integration between frontend and backend components throughout development.

### How the Challenges Were Resolved

* Following a structured Git workflow and version control best practices.
* Collaborating closely when resolving merge conflicts and integrating code changes.
* Holding regular discussions and progress updates to maintain alignment among team members.
* Conducting continuous reviews to ensure consistency in design and user experience.
* Maintaining active coordination between frontend and backend developers throughout the project.
* Performing regular integration testing to verify that frontend and backend components worked together correctly.

Through teamwork, communication, and continuous testing, the team successfully overcame these challenges and maintained steady project progress.

---

## How Can We Improve Collaboration in the Future?

Based on the lessons learned from this project, the team identified several opportunities for improvement:

* Conduct more frequent progress review meetings to identify potential issues earlier and maintain alignment among team members.
* Start project documentation earlier to improve development efficiency and knowledge sharing.
* Increase code review activities before major integrations to improve code quality and reduce integration issues.
* Allocate additional time for testing and quality assurance before deployment.
* Continue maintaining the strong communication, collaboration, and teamwork practices that contributed to the success of this project.

---

## Key Takeaways

The retrospective highlighted the importance of communication, teamwork, role clarity, and proactive project management. By maintaining strong collaboration and continuously improving development practices, the team was able to successfully deliver a complete and functional MVP. The lessons learned from this project will provide valuable guidance for future software development initiatives.
